## Checks the orchestrator asked for
1. **HOME-AC-08 and HOME-AC-10 are met.** There are exactly 4 `<a>` in the svg, each with `href` `#experience`, `#work`, `#writing` or `#contact`. Their `aria-label` and visible text both come from `profile.nav.items` (`CityMap.tsx:50`, `:62`), so the visible label matches the accessible name (WCAG 2.5.3). The page test `page.test.tsx:72` checks every `a[href^="#"]`, the SVG links included, against existing ids. The viewBox is `-418 -303 837 794`, the 9 accents use `var(--accent)` through a class, and no `{{accent}}` or `#ff7a33` remains. **Car grouping is right:** the car is a `g.city-car` with 6 polygons and 2 wheels, painted at its mockup position in the stack. **The sign labels come from content:** `profile.city.signposts` is split 2+2 across the two posts in order (Bengaluru and Manchester, then Trivandrum and Mumbai; C-04), and they are added to the view-source expectations (`content-strings.mjs:40`).
2. **Accessibility.**
   - **Names:** each building is a link named Experience, Work, Writing or Contact, with no img role to hide it. The svg has no role and no name, which is correct because the `<nav>` carries the name.
   - **Visible focus:** `.city-building:focus-visible` sets a 3 px `--focus` outline (`globals.css:652`), lifts the building 4 px and turns the label pill blue. The lift and the pill change alone are a visible indicator. Whether the outline is painted on an SVG `<a>` depends on the engine (Chromium paints it; older WebKit does not). The e2e checks only the computed `outline-width`, and no screenshot shows a focused building (#4).
   - **Landmarks:** two navs, `Main` and the city label. Their names differ, so axe `landmark-unique` would pass. The city name is a 64-character sentence that repeats the hero tip (nit 13).
   - **Tab order:** skip link, header nav (Work, Experience, Writing, Contact), the 2 CTAs, then the map in paint order: Experience (top), Work (right), Writing (left), Contact (bottom). That adds 4 redundant stops and zig-zags visually, but nothing is lost or trapped. Acceptable.
   - **Text at 360 px:** the map renders about 310 px wide (scale about 0.37). Building labels are about **5.6 px** and sign boards about **4.8 px**, and they are unreadable in `screenshots/map-360.png` (#3). AA has no minimum text size, and the same names are in the header nav, so this is minor.
   - **Target size:** building bounding boxes at 360 px are about 60–110 px across, well over 24 px.
   - **Highlight contrast:** 6.70:1, passes.
3. **Motion CSS.** `transition: transform 150ms ease-out` on the link and `fill 150ms ease-out` on the pill (`globals.css:631-640`). `translateY(-4px)` on an SVG `<a>`: a pure translation, so `transform-box` and `transform-origin` do not matter. CSS `px` maps to user units in the SVG's coordinate system, so the lift is 4 viewBox units (about 1.5 CSS px at 360 px; acceptable, nit 15). The car: `12s ease-out 1 both`, with a compositor-only transform and no layout cost. Its start offset `(-180,-104)` follows the road's slope (0.578), but the start point (about −276, 74) is off the ground tile, so for the first seconds the car floats in the mint background (nit 12). **Reduced motion** is the global `* , ::before, ::after { animation: none !important; transition: none !important }` (`globals.css:709-715`). This leaves 0 animations and 0 s transitions everywhere. The hover and focus rules are not inside the media query, so the label colour and the (now instant) lift still apply. The rule ships only with the `(site)` root layout, and the terminal link is a plain `<a>` (full navigation), so it does not leak into `/terminal`.
4. **CLS and LCP.** The svg is inline in the server HTML with a viewBox, `width: 100%; height: auto` and `aspect-ratio: 837 / 794` (`globals.css:620-625`), so its box is known at first layout (no CLS). SVG content is not an LCP candidate, so the LCP stays the `h1`. Cost: about 16 KB more inline markup in the HTML, not render-blocking. At 1440 px the map sits beside the copy, and at 360 px it wraps below the tip, with the first-viewport items above the fold (`screenshots/hero-360.png`). The Lighthouse report-only numbers in the DoD are not recorded yet (#7).
5. **e2e specs.** No spec can pass vacuously on its main assertion: counts are bounded (`total >= 14`, `seen.length === total`), the car filter must equal exactly one entry, and the reduced-motion test asserts 0 animations. There are some weak points:
   - Fixed `waitForTimeout(250)` and `(200)` sleeps after 150 ms transitions. 100 ms of margin is enough locally, but it is a classic CI flake source (nit 10).
   - The 2.5.8 spacing check skips the circle-against-circle case (nit 11).
   - The CLS test measures the ratio after load, so it would pass without `aspect-ratio` too (nit 14).
   - The outline check reads computed style, not painted pixels (#4).
   - `getAnimations()` keeps returning the car after 12 s because of `fill-mode: both`, so that assertion is not timing-sensitive.
6. **Gate integrity.**
   - **`check-literals`:** the digit exemption widens more than SVG data. Any token with a letter and a digit (`bet365`, `4px`, `U-08`) stops counting as a word, so short copy that contains one now slips under the 3-word limit (#6, probe above). The new test does not cover this edge: its "copy beside numbers" sample has `9+` and `2016`, which have no letters and were never counted.
   - **View-source:** the signposts are added correctly. The building labels are already expected through `nav.items`.

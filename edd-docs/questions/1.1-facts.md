**How to answer:** for each row, fill **Owner answer** with one of:

- `confirm`: publish exactly as written.
- `edit: <new text>`: publish your corrected text instead.
- `remove`: do not publish.

**Source:** `docs/design/home-reference/Main.dc.html` (written `L<n>`). "Spec" means `docs/design/REDESIGN_SPEC.md`.

- `NUM`: contains a number or metric.
- `NDA`: bet365-related; the spec allows no internal metrics, architecture or vendor names beyond what you confirm here.
- `PH`: a placeholder the spec designates for you (email, notice period, Opteamix bullets) or a `[TO MEASURE]` slot. It renders as omitted or "Not yet measured" until supplied.
- `VISA`: visa or immigration status. The spec says never to invent this.
- `PRIV`: personal or family information.
- `MISSING`: the mockup lacks this, but the spec needs it. Please supply it.
- `CHK`: possible inconsistency for you to check.
